- **Severity:** Major
- **Priority:** High
- **Environment:** [browser, OS, date]

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Sweet Shop website and make sure the cart is empty
2. Click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner
3. Type: "Check my cart"
